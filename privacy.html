Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This policy explains how Verified Facts Daily Studio ("the app"), a private tool run by the owner of the Verified Facts Daily channel, handles data. The app is used only by the channel owner to manage his own YouTube channel and Instagram account.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's privacy practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the app accesses

  • Upload access (youtube.upload): to upload videos to the owner's own channel, only when the owner clicks "upload" or "schedule".
  • Read-only access (youtube.readonly): the owner's channel name and ID, and view, like and comment counts of the owner's own videos, to show statistics to the owner.
  • Instagram (Instagram API with Instagram Login): the owner's username and account ID, and permission to publish Reels to the owner's own account when the owner chooses to.

The app does not access, collect or store data about other YouTube or Instagram users, viewers or commenters.

How data is stored and used

  • Access tokens and the statistics above are stored only on the owner's private server, protected so only the app can read them.
  • The data is used only to upload the owner's videos and to show the owner how his videos perform.
  • Data is never sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Retention and deletion

Statistics are kept while the channel uses the app. Disconnecting YouTube or Instagram in the app deletes the stored access token. You can also revoke the app's access at any time at Google Account → Third-party apps, which stops all access immediately. To ask for deletion of any stored data, email kevinjobb09@gmail.com; it will be deleted within 30 days.

Cookies

This website and the app do not use tracking cookies.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.
